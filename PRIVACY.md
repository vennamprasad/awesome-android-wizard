# Privacy Policy for Awesome Android Wizard

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 7, 2026  

Awesome Android Wizard ("the Plugin") is developed and maintained by Prasad Vennam ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

---

### 1. No Data Collection
Awesome Android Wizard operates **entirely offline and locally** on your machine:
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or account credentials.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking**: The Plugin does not contain analytics SDKs, trackers, or telemetry reporting tools.
- **No Code Transmission**: Your source code, project files, and configurations never leave your local machine. All scaffolding and diagnostic operations execute strictly on your local disk within Android Studio / IntelliJ IDEA.

---

### 2. Network Activity
The Plugin itself does **not** initiate background network connections or transmit data to external servers. Any network operations (such as downloading Gradle distributions or dependencies) are triggered directly by your local Gradle process or Android Studio, subject to your own network and IDE configurations.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The Plugin does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, or data collection services.

---

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this repository.
